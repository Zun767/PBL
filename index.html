Popular Burmses Food

Moh Hin Gah

Mohinga, Myanmar's national dish, is a flavorful rice noodle soup with a rich, aromatic fish-based broth, often thickened with crushed rice or chickpea flour. Topped with boiled eggs, crispy fritters, and coriander, it’s a versatile meal enjoyed any time of day.

Kyauk Kyaw

Kyauk Kyaw is a traditional Burmese snack made with coconut milk, sugar, and agar-agar. This refreshing dessert is slightly sweet and features two layers: a creamy white coconut layer and a jelly layer.

View Recipe

Tea Leaf Salad

Tea Leaf Salad, or Lahpet Thoke, is a popular Burmese dish made with fermented tea leaves, mixed with peanuts, sesame seeds, garlic, tomatoes, and chili for a tangy, crunchy flavor.

View Recipe

Nan Gyi Thoke

Nan Gyi Thoke is a classic Burmese (Myanmar) dish, often referred to as "Burmese spaghetti salad." It’s a dry noodle salad made with thick rice noodles and coated in a flavorful, aromatic curry sauce. It’s a popular comfort food in Myanmar and can be eaten as a breakfast or lunch dish.
